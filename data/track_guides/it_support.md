# IT Support

## Overview
IT support helps people keep computers, software, accounts, and networks usable. The work ranges from answering basic questions to diagnosing recurring technical problems. Entry-level practice combines clear communication with careful troubleshooting: gather symptoms, test a likely cause, document the result, and escalate issues that require more access or expertise. The goal is reliable service, not simply making a quick change.

## Core skills
Ticket triage; operating-system basics; account and access support; hardware and peripheral troubleshooting; networking fundamentals; clear documentation; customer communication; privacy-aware handling of information.

## Typical entry-level roles
Help desk technician, service desk analyst, desktop support technician, and IT support specialist are common role titles. Duties vary by organization and may combine user assistance with device setup or account administration.

## What each stage means for this track
### Foundations
Learn computer components, operating-system navigation, files, accounts, and common network terms. Practice asking focused questions before changing settings.
### Build Skills
Work through repeatable troubleshooting scenarios, use command-line utilities carefully, and document each test and result. Explain technical steps in plain language.
### Prove Skills
Demonstrate a ticket workflow: reproduce an issue, isolate a cause, apply an appropriate fix, verify it, and write concise notes.
### Apply
Prepare examples of patient communication, methodical problem solving, and safe escalation. Review role requirements and apply when experience and eligibility match.

## Common beginner mistakes
Guessing at causes, changing several settings at once, or closing a ticket before checking whether the problem is resolved can make troubleshooting harder. Avoid recording passwords or unnecessary personal details. Do not treat every issue as a device problem; clarify symptoms and check connectivity, account status, and recent changes. Escalate promptly when permissions or safety requirements exceed your role.
